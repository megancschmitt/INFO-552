After a career in historic preservation, I decided to pursue a long-time goal of becoming a librarian. I began my MLIS degree in 2020 has have been taking classes while working full time in the Department of Architecture at the University of Pennsylvania's Stuart Weitzman School of Design.

In September of 2024 I was hired to work at the Athenaeum of Philadelphia as an Assistant Librarian. It has been a dream come true, only solidifying how excited I am to find the opportunity to do this work full time.

I am originally from Philadelphia and I think this may have contributed to my lifelong love of architecture. That is why I pursued a career in historic preservation. I had the luck and privilege of working for the cities of New York, Miami and Philadelphia at their municipal preservation commissions. It was an exciting, challenging and important time for me, and I learned so much. However, I started to feel like I was no longer growing as a person. As soon as I began the MLIS program at Drexel, I knew I had found my next professional chapter.

The Athenaeum is a perfect place for me due to its extensive focus on architecture, architectural history, and topics related to historic preservation. It feels like a perfect match!
