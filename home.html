So You're Looking For A Summer Job....?

Ready Set Work!

This website will help Philadelphia teenagers understand all of the steps needed to find your first summer job. We will help you navigate how to apply for the required work permissions and look for a job opportunity so you start building your out-of-school skill set! Ready Set Work! believes that young people can learn just as much from the right summer job as they can from the classroom.
