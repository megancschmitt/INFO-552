The Commonwealth of Pennsylvania allows children between the ages of 14 - 17 to work but only with a Work Permit. You can learn more about the Pennsylvania Child Labor Law here.

You will need to work with the School District of Philadelphia to get your Work Permit.

From August 27, 2024, through June 12, 2025, between 3PM-5PM, you can apply for your Work Permit in person at the following locations:

  • West Philadelphia High School, 4901 Chestnut St, Philadelphia, PA 19139 TUESDAYS ONLY
  • Philadelphia High School, 2101 S Broad St, Philadelphia, PA 19148 WEDNESDAYS ONLY
  • High School, 3201 Ryan Ave, Philadelphia, PA 19136 THURSDAYS ONLY
